## 1. Brand Core & Value Proposition

### 1.1 Brand Story & Essence
- **Brand Name:** Azhai (Tamil: *அழை* — An Invitation; also signifies *Inherent Grace & Beauty*).
- **Core Narrative:** *"You are not borrowing someone else's beauty. You are simply returning to your own."*
- **Founder:** Preethi — bridging Sri Lankan artisanal handloom heritage with clean, contemporary silhouettes.
- **Visual Identity:**
  - **Sacred Maroon (`#701626`):** Auspicious wedding silk threads, timeless festive grandeur.
  - **Temple Gold (`#C5A059` / `#DFBF77`):** Warm shimmer of brass oil lamps and handloom zari borders.
  - **Natural Cream (`#FCFBF8` / `#F7F4EE`):** Unbleached raw cotton, breathable purity.
- **Logomark Symbolism:**
  - *The Sweeping Initial 'A':* The single, unbroken thread of artisan handloom craft from weaver's loom to wardrobe.
  - *The Blooming Lotus:* Purity, rebirth, and modern feminine poise.

### 1.2 Core Differentiators (Why Buy from Azhai?)
1. **Flattering South Asian Tailoring:** Silhouettes designed specifically for South Asian body types, balancing breathable comfort with waist-defining structure.
2. **Dual Model: Ready-to-Wear + Bespoke Studio:** Customers can choose ready-to-ship sizes (S to XXL) or enter exact custom body measurements for custom-stitched pieces.
3. **Transparent Artisanal Heritage:** Breathable handloom cottons, mulberry silks, and lightweight organzas built to outlast fleeting fast-fashion cycles.
4. **Risk-Free Sri Lankan Checkout:** Island-wide Cash on Delivery (COD) via Sri Lanka Post Speed Post, direct local bank transfers with instant verification, and prompt WhatsApp assistance.

## 6. Influencer & UGC Collaboration Playbook

### 6.1 Influencer Selection Criteria
Do not chase vanity follower counts. Focus on Colombo, Jaffna, and diaspora micro-creators (5k – 35k followers) who demonstrate:
- High engagement rate (> 4% comment-to-view ratio).
- An aesthetic aligned with refined minimalism, traditional roots, and modern elegance.
- Audiences of South Asian young women, professionals, and wedding attendees.

## 10. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) & Analytics

### Tracking Infrastructure
- **Google Tag Manager:** Container `GTM-5JFH9Q8R` deployed on production with automated triggers.
- **Conversion Events to Monitor:**
  - `view_item` (Product view)
  - `add_to_cart` (Cart additions)
  - `begin_checkout` (Checkout initiated)
  - `purchase` (Order confirmed via Bank Transfer or COD)

### Performance Benchmarks
| Metric | Benchmark Target | Action if Below Target |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Blended ROAS (Return on Ad Spend)** | **3.5x – 5.0x** | Refresh hook in first 3s of video; refine audience to Colombo + high-intent towns. |
| **E-commerce Conversion Rate** | **2.0% – 3.2%** | Check checkout friction, promote COD more prominently, verify mobile load speed. |
| **Average Order Value (AOV)** | **LKR 7,500 – 12,000** | Introduce cross-sells (e.g. matching dupattas, pairing kurti with trousers). |
| **Cart Abandonment Rate** | **< 65%** | Activate 1-hour Brevo email + WhatsApp concierge follow-up. |
| **Return / Exchange Rate** | **< 4%** | Ensure accurate bust/waist measurement guides and fabric zoom previews. |
